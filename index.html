Research Objective

Language models, reasoning, and human‑AI teaming

My research studies the interplay between large language models (LLMs) and reasoning, with a focus on the role of intermediate tokens in performance and behavior. I also design robust, scalable frameworks for Human‑AI collaboration, complementing modern LLMs with principled planners from the AI Planning community.
